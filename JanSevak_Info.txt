1. ABOUT JANSEVAK
==================================================

JanSevak is a civic participation ecosystem that connects citizens, government authorities, and NGOs to report, resolve, and track local sanitation and sustainability issues.

It is not just another complaint app. It closes the full loop:

Report -> Validate -> Guide -> Act -> Hold Accountable -> Reward -> Learn

==================================================
2. THE PROBLEM
==================================================

Today's civic complaint systems face several challenges:

1. Reports disappear without feedback, causing citizens to lose trust.
2. Authorities struggle to distinguish urgent issues from less critical reports, resulting in slow and inefficient responses.
3. Citizens often do not know which problems they can solve themselves.
4. NGOs frequently work in isolation, leading to wasted resources.
5. A lack of incentives and accountability can cause issues to remain unresolved for extended periods, sometimes more than 30 days.

3. KEY FEATURES
==================================================

3.1. Hyper-Local Geo-Layered Feed

- Displays civic issues within a default 500-meter radius.
- Allows users to zoom out to ward, city, district, and state levels.
- Supports filtering by category, status, severity, and action type.

3.2. Community Validation

- Allows users to confirm that they have seen an issue.
- Enables citizens to indicate how an issue affects them.
- Lets community members volunteer to help.
- Supports in-person verification.
- Includes severity polls and credibility-weighted voting.
- Calculates an Urgency Score to help trigger escalation.

3.3. AI-Powered Reporting

- Analyzes photos to identify waste types, hazards, and severity.
- Supports voice-to-text reporting.
- Generates report titles and descriptions automatically.
- Aims to make reporting possible in approximately two minutes.

3.4. Intelligent Chatbot

- Provides a 24/7 multilingual assistant.
- Supports Hindi, English, and regional languages.
- Diagnoses problems through natural conversation.
- Escalates critical cases and provides step-by-step guidance.

3.5. Dual-Action Framework

TYPE A - COMMUNITY-SOLVABLE
Citizens can address the issue themselves with appropriate guidance.

TYPE B - COMMUNITY-SUPPORTED
Citizens and government authorities work in parallel.

TYPE C - GOVERNMENT-PRIMARY
Government authorities lead the response while citizens report and monitor progress.

TYPE D - EMERGENCY
Emergency response takes priority. Citizens receive guidance to stay safe, evacuate when necessary, and report the incident when safe to do so.

3.6. Machine Learning Impact Prediction

- Forecasts the probability of success for proposed actions.
- Compares community-led, government-led, and hybrid approaches.
- Learns from actual outcomes to improve future predictions.

3.7. Rewards and Incentives

- Awards points for reporting issues.
- Rewards community organizing and participation.
- Encourages citizens to monitor the progress of reported issues.
- Supports reward redemption through the platform.

4. PROJECT VISION
==================================================

JanSevak aims to build a transparent, accountable, and collaborative civic ecosystem where citizens can participate in solving local problems and authorities can respond more effectively.

Our goal is to turn civic complaints into measurable community action and lasting environmental improvement.
